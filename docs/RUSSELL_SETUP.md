# Bullseye intake form — Russell's one-page guide

The picture version of this page, for Russell's phone: **https://chiranr19.github.io/bullseye-intake/guide.html**
(`guide.html` in the repo; self-contained, can be emailed as a file).

## 1. Your link

**https://chiranr19.github.io/bullseye-intake/?ref=text**

Say it out loud as "chiran r nineteen dot github dot io, slash bullseye dash intake". But you will
almost never say it. You text it.

**Make it one word to type.** Then replying to a customer is: type `formlink`, hit send.

- **iPhone:** Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement → tap **+**.
  Phrase: paste the link above. Shortcut: `formlink`. Save.
- **Android (Gboard):** Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard → Gboard →
  Dictionary → Personal dictionary → **+**. Word: paste the link. Shortcut: `formlink`.
- **Either phone, simplest of all:** keep the link in a note and copy it.

What to text back when someone asks about a chip: *"Sure — fill this in and I'll text you a time:
formlink"*

## 2. At the car

Two ways, pick whichever is handier:

- **Show the QR code.** Save `qr/russell.png` to your phone's photos (or use the laminated card).
  Customer opens their camera, points at it, taps the link that pops up.
- **Hand them your phone.** Open **https://chiranr19.github.io/bullseye-intake/?ref=russell**
  on your own phone and hand it over. They fill it in and sign on your screen.

Only name, phone, and signature are required. If they skip the insurance card or the VIN, you
get it at the car like always.

## 3. Where it all goes

**Email.** Every submission sends you one email. The subject line tells you the job at a glance:

> New repair: Jane Doe — 2019 Honda Civic — State Farm

If the car or insurer says **TBD**, they skipped it. The insurance card photo is attached.
Links at the bottom open the sheet and the customer's folder (photos and signature).

**The sheet.** Open the **Google Sheets** app on your phone → **Bullseye Customers**. One row
per customer, newest at the bottom. Change **Status** as the job moves along:
New → Contacted → Claim filed → Scheduled → Repaired → Billed → Paid (or Cancelled).
New rows are yellow, Paid rows are green. The columns from **Claim #** onward are yours to fill in
however you like. Tap the **Folder** link in a row to see that customer's photos and signature.

## 4. If a customer can't or won't

Paper is still fine. Type their row into the sheet later, or don't. Nothing depends on the form.

## 5. If something breaks

Text Chiran. Nothing you do in the sheet can break the form: edit, sort, color, delete rows,
whatever you like. Only the header row (row 1) should stay as it is.

## 6. Printing QR codes

All in the `qr/` folder. Each one is the same form; the name just tells us where the scan came from.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `flyer.png` | the paper flyer |
| `truck.png` | the truck magnet or decal |
| `russell.png` | your phone or a laminated card to show at the car |
| `web.png` | a website or online listing |

Print at 2 inches wide or bigger. Black on white, leave the white border around it alone.
